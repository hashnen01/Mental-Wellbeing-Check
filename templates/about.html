About This Project

Purpose

Wellbeing Check is a simple tool that estimates your overall Health & Wellbeing Score based on your daily lifestyle habits.

It looks at factors like how well you sleep, how active you are, what you eat, your social connections, and your work-life balance. These are then combined into a single score between 0 and 100.

The goal is to help you understand how your everyday choices affect your overall wellbeing, and to give you practical suggestions for improvement.

How Predictions Are Made

This application uses a Machine Learning regression model to estimate the wellbeing score. The process works as follows:

  1. Data Collection: Your responses to the assessment form are collected as input features.
  2. Feature Engineering: Five composite scores are calculated — Sleep Quality, Physical Activity, Nutrition, Social Connection, and Daily Balance.
  3. Prediction: The trained model processes all features and outputs a wellbeing score between 0 and 100.
  4. Explanation: The system identifies which factors contributed the most to your score, both positively and negatively.
  5. Recommendations: Based on the factors that are lowering your score, personalised and practical suggestions are generated.

Important Disclaimer

This application is designed for educational purposes and is not a medical diagnostic tool. It does not diagnose depression, anxiety, or any medical condition. It simply provides a lifestyle-based wellbeing assessment. If you have concerns about your mental health, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
